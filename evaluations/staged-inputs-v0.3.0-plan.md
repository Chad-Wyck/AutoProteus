# v0.3.0: Qwen 9B staged-input comparison

Prepared before live inference on 20 October 2026. This is a directed second
extension experiment, not a demonstration of autonomous first-and-second growth.

- Qwen3.5 9B Q6_K, cached verified weights, PrismML CUDA runtime, GTX 1080 Ti.
- Standard production native adapter, 131,072 context, 4,096 response tokens,
  thinking off, temperature 0.6, top-p 0.8, top-k 20, Q8 KV cache. Preserve exact
  launch arguments, weight/runtime hashes, provider traces and plan fingerprints.
- One exact supplied hashing-capable parent from the previous v0.3.0 study.
  Its model-written implementation and tests are unchanged. Recheck the parent
  independently before inference; it earns no autonomous first-upgrade credit.
- Add `format_json`, preserve hashing, then format the supplied inventory and
  write its audit receipt. The existing independent contracts remain unchanged.
- Three repeats per arm, alternating baseline/treatment, treatment/baseline,
  baseline/treatment. The baseline sees the real final input during development
  and must preserve it until activation. The treatment receives a disposable
  example; the controller supplies the real input only after verification.
- Both arms share 128 actions, 65,536 generated tokens and 1,800 seconds across
  development, grading, activation and final use. No extra activation allowance,
  operator candidate edits, hidden retries or automatic protocol fallback.
- Admission requires successful bounded app connection qualification, the
  runner's benchmark gate, and at least four of five ordinary tasks. Record
  first-pass qualification and repaired qualification separately. A failure
  stops dependent trials and remains in the evidence.

Report independently checked extensions, retention, submission/activation,
correct final artifacts, explicit completion and full workflows separately.
Input preservation and withheld-input protocol compliance are different
measures. Audit saved final artifacts independently even when workflow credit
fails; this does not rewrite the original verdict. Compare costs only with
explicit measurement coverage, and distinguish logical input tokens from
uncached compute.

This is a six-attempt comparison with one model, familiar contract and supplied
parent. No significance, generalization or first-pass growth claim follows from
three repeats. Other models and fresh first/second chains remain later work.
The editable seed and normal app defaults remain frozen throughout this batch.

Private evidence: `.data/staged-inputs-v030-20261020/`. Results will be saved
separately; preparing this plan does not start model inference.
