## Failure analysis and controller limitation

This pre-activation difference cannot establish that activation placement caused the 3/3 versus 1/3 first-stage result. Every first candidate that passed independent implementation checks also completed its hashing task after activation, in both arms.

All **four of four** second-pass candidates passed every held-out hashing behavior check. Three aggregate retention verdicts were false because the probe also runs the entire candidate test suite, including failing new inventory tests. Those verdicts must not be described as loss of the earlier hashing capability. All four second candidates passed the protected starter suite.

Inventory defects included empty listings, paths relative to the listed directory instead of the workspace, a missing `os` import, unwanted recursion, missing strict boolean checks, and truncating results where the contract requires rejecting more than 200 files. Two candidates also returned an extra `truncated` field, which fails the frozen exact-object checks; both independently violated the explicit over-limit rejection requirement, so these were not merely formatting-only failures.

The last activation-history second pass stopped at **115 actions** with `Invalid model request`. Its final well-formed neutral request contained **402 messages** and 237,983 encoded bytes, below the byte ceiling but above the broker's separate **400-message ceiling**. Record this as a **host-limited trajectory**, with 13 nominal actions left, rather than an unimpeded model failure. Its saved candidate still failed inventory checks, but whether remaining actions would have repaired it is unknown. The frozen observed 0/3 completion score is retained and the limitation is flagged separately. No retry or extra allowance was substituted.

Four stages reached the action ceiling. None reached the 60-minute or generated-token ceilings.
